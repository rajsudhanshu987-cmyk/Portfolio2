02 — About

A short, honest version of the story so far.

I am a third-year computer science student spending most of my hours reading machine learning papers and shipping the ideas that survive. My interests sit at the seam of probabilistic modeling, applied deep learning, and tooling that makes research reproducible. I work openly on GitHub, contribute to libraries I rely on, and write occasionally about what I learn.

I started in 2021 with a curiosity about how recommendation feeds learn what we like. Two years later I was building my own — badly — and that gap between what I could imagine and what I could ship became the engine for everything that followed.

Today my focus is narrower and sharper: applied deep learning, reproducible research tooling, and the kind of full-stack craft that lets a model travel from notebook to user without losing its soul.
